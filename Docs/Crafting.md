# Crafting and the action bar

## Crafting window (laid out like Throne and Liberty's)

Talk to a class vendor in St. Revere and press **Craft** in its window. The crafting window opens:

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left: category tabs | Weapons, Head, Chest, Feet, Potions, Other (only the tabs with something in them) |
| Middle: the list | Everything the vendor makes in that category, lowest level first, ten per page (`<` `>`). Gear shows its levels; "Materials ready" when you can make it now |
| Right: the chosen piece | Its name and stats, its level (`<` `>` picks another level of the same piece; it starts at the best level you can wear), the **Crafting Material** list (have / need, red when short) |
| Quantity | `10` `30` `50` `100`, `-` and `+` (1–100) |
| Craft | Shows the vendor's fee (a tenth of the item's price each). Under it: "Insufficient crafting materials", "Requires level N" or "Not enough gold" |

- **Gear** is made from the materials monsters drop (each piece: its material × 6 for weapons and chests, × 4 for heads and
  feet). Every crafted piece may come out better (gold 2 %, blue 8 %, green 25 %) and with a socket (20 %).
- **Potions and Other** are the source game's crafting recipes (`crafting_recipes`, generated by
  `Tools/GameData/recipes.py` → `Scripts/Editor/RecipeData.cs`) whose items all exist here: 5 potions of a tier make one of
  the next, 5 Major Potions make a Major Serum, 5 Major orbs + 2 Magic Dust make a Divine orb, Magic Dust + Inactive
  Demiplane Key + Major Treasury Gold make the Demiplane Key.
- The server checks everything: standing at the vendor, level, materials, gold and room in the bag
  (`Inventory.ServerCraft`, `Inventory.ServerCraftRecipe`).

## Action bar

Like the source game's bar: **12 slots** on keys **1–9, 0, - and =**. A slot holds **Attack**, a **spell** or a **usable
item** (potions show how many are left; when a potion runs out, the best one of the same kind is used).

- A new character: Attack on 1, the first spells on 2, 3, 4, the healing and mana potions on 5 and 6.
- **Drag** a spell from the spellbook (P) or a usable item from the bag (I) onto a slot.
- **Drag** a slot onto another slot to swap them; drag it **off the bar** to empty it.
- Clicking a slot does the same as its key. (Picking a spell in the spellbook and clicking a slot still works.)
- **F** still attacks the selected target wherever Attack sits.
- The bar is saved with the character; bars saved before (7 spell slots) keep their spells on the same keys.

Tests: `SpellbookTests` (bar), `VendorTests` (crafting).
