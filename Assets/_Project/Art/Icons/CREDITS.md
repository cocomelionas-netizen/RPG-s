# Icons

`Icons.png` is packed by `Tools/Art/icons.py` from the art below. Using it in this project means following these
licenses: credit the authors, and share changes to the CC-BY-SA art under the same license.

## Items: Flare (CC-BY-SA 3.0)

The weapon, armor, jewelry, gem, potion, scroll, book, key and coin icons and the crafting icons (crystals, sack,
herbs) are from the open game **Flare** (flare-game, https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game:
`mods/fantasycore/images/icons/icons.png`, `mods/empyrean_campaign/images/icons/`), CC-BY-SA 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Per-file authors: https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game/wiki/Credits
(the item icons are mostly by Clint Bellanger and the Flare team).

## Spells and orbs: Painterly Spell Icons (CC-BY-SA 3.0)

By **J. W. Bjerk (eleazzaar)**, www.jwbjerk.com/art, https://opengameart.org/content/painterly-spell-icons-part-1
(parts 1-4), CC-BY-SA 3.0 (also GPL 2.0/3.0, CC-BY 3.0). Scaled from 256 to 64 pixels.
